TASK:
Generate lesson package from evidence.

INPUT:
- evidence/<LESSON_ID>.md (single source of truth)
- templates/lesson_template.mdx
- templates/quiz_schema.json

OUTPUTS:
1) content/<track>/modules/<MODULE_ID>/lessons/<LESSON_ID>.mdx
2) content/<track>/modules/<MODULE_ID>/lessons/<LESSON_ID>.quiz.json
3) content/<track>/modules/<MODULE_ID>/lessons/<LESSON_ID>.lab.md

MDX RULES:
- Must include exactly the 6 sections defined in CLAUDE.md.
- Include 2 examples (if Java/Spring) or 2 queries (if SQL).
- Must be consistent with evidence.
- If a claim is not supported, add TODO rather than invent.

QUIZ RULES:
- 10 questions default:
  - 6 single_choice, 2 code_output, 2 short_answer
- Every explanation must cite evidence tags: (Evidence: E3, E9)
- All questions original.

LAB RULES:
- 30–45 min
- Specify exact class/method (or SQL tables/expected result)
- Designed for auto-grading
